Brett Williams

Brett is an accomplished and versatile librarian based in Toronto, with over a decade of experience across a broad range of library services and academic disciplines. He holds a Master of Library and Information Studies (MLIS) from McGill University and began his career establishing the library systems for the College of the North Atlantic-Qatar.

Publications

  • Research, Scanning and Tool Building All in a Special Library? We've Got It "covid-ed"! — OLA 2021, February 3, 2021 (with Karen L.). Ontario Medical Association librarians on streamlining and integrating research, scanning and tool building, leveraging technology and modifying workflow, and customizing cloud-based research tools to address challenges presented during COVID-19.
  • Bibleometrics, Finding and Academic Misconduct — Dimensions and Altmetric Workshop, May 28, 2020.
  • Dimensions & VOSViewer Bibliometrics in the Reference Interview — Code 4 Lib Journal, February 17, 2020. VOSviewer bibliometric mapping using Dimensions, Scopus and Web of Science data; advanced searches in Dimensions, citation-data export, and using the resulting maps in reference interviews for graduate students, faculty, and post-PhD researchers.
  • What You Don't See — OLA Superconference 2019, February 1, 2019. Two academic librarians who worked where they had to navigate censorship, surveillance, and human rights concerns discuss what they learned and how it changed the way they view their profession.
  • Confessions of a Scan Artist — OLA Superconference 2019, January 31, 2019. Two librarians at the nexus of academic and special libraries on building a scanning service in a special library, including workflow, copyright, equipment and software, and scanning for patrons with disabilities.
  • How to Manage Libtech Innovation With Wardley Value Chain Mapping — Computers in Libraries, October 2017. Wardley value chain mapping as a simple, effective technology-planning tool, with case studies, usable across organizations and for IT strategy planning without extensive training.
  • Wardley Value Chain Mapping in Library Technology — Computers in Libraries 2017, March 2017. An introduction to Wardley Value Chain Mapping for planning and communicating the evolution of services and technologies, identifying opportunities for disruption and innovation, and managing library technology.
  • Reference in the Wild: Personal and Professional Use of Quora and Stack Exchange — OLA Superconference 2017, February 2, 2017. Question and answer websites as a tool for building reference skills, assisting users in searching and asking questions, and use in reference, instruction, and professional development.
  • International Perspectives on Information Literacy: A Panel Discussion — Georgia International Information Literacy Conference, October 10, 2014 (with Kayo Chang Black). Three Canadian academic librarians — one in Canada, one in Hong Kong, and one in Qatar — on what information literacy competency and education means for them and their students.
  • "Officially Speaking": The Latter-day Saint Documentary Opposition to the Equal Rights Amendment — Research in Religious Studies Student Conference 2006, February 2006. An exposition and analysis of official statements from the Salt Lake City-based Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints opposing the proposed Equal Rights Amendment to the US constitution.
